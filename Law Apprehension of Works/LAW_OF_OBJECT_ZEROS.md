## Statement

Every object is the zero of its own frame. Four verified pieces:

1. SELF-GAUGE (A0, [VERIFIED] math): an object measures the world relative to
   itself — reading n(u) = s/u, unit-invariant. "Zero" is what an object is to
   itself: the datum (r = 0, degree 0) of its own coordinates. There is no
   privileged gauge.
2. RADIUS OF VISIBILITY (L03/L2, [PROVEN], checks 17-18): the same object reads
   as a point (degree 0) above its last degree-survival rank. Its own "zero"
   is a zero of resolvability too — beyond its visibility radius, the object
   is indistinguishable from a point.
3. OWN HORIZON (L10/E15, [PROVEN]/[ESTABLISHED]): every observer's observable
   ball is centered on itself (radius = particle horizon 4.4e26 m for us),
   and every observer's ball is isotropic with the same kind of horizon (E16).
4. NO SHARED ZERO (E05, [ESTABLISHED]): nothing in the verified ladder elevates
   any object to a privileged zero; there is no universal center (and,
   symmetrically, no edge — E14).

So the claimable content of "object zeros" is a statement about coordinates and
resolvability: the universe has as many centers as it has objects/observers,
and none of them is privileged. This is the recursion of L11 stated as a law of
frames.
