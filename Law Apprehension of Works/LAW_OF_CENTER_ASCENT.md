## PART II — THE LAW (axioms, in full)

P1 THE DATUM. There is one center, the Great Attractor, and it is the floor.
   Axiom: in its own gauge, position = 0 and size = 0 forever (self-gauge lemma).
   Consequence: the datum is the null reading of every lattice, the reference for
   all ascent. No scale exists below it.
   [REAL anchor: a massive concentration ~50 Mpc toward Norma, heart of Laniakea,
   Local Group drawn toward it at a few hundred km/s.]

P2 THE GROWTH. The center grows: its tick u(t) increases monotonically.
   Consequence: the same physical span spans fewer ticks over time (n(u) falls),
   so structures are "read smaller" at later epochs - the ladder stretches.
   The datum's own reading stays 0 while doing so (fresh lattice, A0), hence a
   "growing zero": the growth is invisible at the center, visible only as ascent.
   [REAL anchor: attractor mass scale ~10^16 M_sun (lensing/X-ray). GROWTH of the
   attractor itself in time: POSTULATE - no epoch-comparison measurement exists.]

P3 THE ASCENT. Matter is pushed up from the datum as high as it can reach.
   Consequence: an object rests exactly on the highest rung where n >= 1; above
   that rung it becomes a point (reading < 1) and is "as high as it can go".
   [DIRECTION-FLIP POSTULATE vs standard cosmology: established cosmic repulsion
   is outward expansion at Hubble shear; ascent is a push from the floor upward.
   Not observationally established; kept as an axiom, labeled.]

P4 THE LOCK. Galaxies lock to galaxies: matter binds pair-to-pair up the ladder.
   Consequence: structures collapse into bound hierarchies (pairs, groups,
   clusters, superclusters bound against local shear), each lock topping the one
   below. A lock fails exactly where expansion shear exceeds binding.
   [REAL anchor: bound systems are measured - Local Group bound against the
   Hubble flow; Andromeda-Milky Way approaching ~110 km/s; bound pairs/groups and
   dynamical friction. The word "lock" names real gravitation, nothing invented.]

P5 THE TORNADO. At the very start there is a tornado of creation - the primal
   spiral, the pi/2 turn-walk (node C1), opened at t = 0.
   Consequence: handedness is set once at the start; every later rotation inherits
its direction. The spiral is open - r^2 = (sum E-W)^2 + (sum N-S)^2 of the
    turn lengths, NOT the sum of their squares (corrected in place,
    corrigendum 74; nested rings were removed by test, see linkage note) - so
    the creation is not closed loops but one growing spiral.
   [REAL anchor: rotation is pervasive (galaxy spins, vortex structures). The
   PRIMAL tornado at the origin of structure: POSTULATE - no primordial vortex is
   observed. Direction inheritance: POSTULATE.]

## PART III — LAW-INTERNAL DERIVATIONS (theorems of the law, not physics)

L1 THE STAYS-ZERO THEOREM. The datum's reading is 0 at every epoch:
   n_GA(u(t)) = 0 for all t. Proof: by P1 the datum is its own gauge; a fresh
   lattice cannot import a tick into it. The growth (P2) adds ticks without
   adding extent. So the center climbs every rung of the ladder while remaining
   size-nothing - the only "matter" with unbounded height.

L2 THE RESOLUTION BOUND. Ascent height h_O satisfies h_O = sup { u : n_O(u) >= 1 }:
   an object cannot stand on a rung whose tick exceeds its own diameter; above
   that rung its reading is < 1 and it is a point (node F1). For the ball,
   D = 550 nm -> highest rung near u ~ 550 nm; below ~um it is a colloid,
   above ~um a point (entry 70 boundary, verified).
   Corollary L2a: the ball can never "stand" on the rungs of Earth, Laniakea, or
   the GA: readings 8.62e-14, ~1e-28..., < 1 - it is point-material there.

L3 THE LOCK CONDITION. A lock binds iff binding energy exceeds the expansion
   shear across the pair: U_bind(t) > |Hubble differential| over the separation.
   When it binds, relative velocity turns infall; when it fails, the pair
   recedes. The hierarchy of locks = the ladder of bound structures (P4).
   [REAL: Local Group is bound; galaxy recession above ~1-2 Mpc separation
   returns to the Hubble flow - the observed border of the lock.]

L4 THE TORNADO PRIORITY. All rotation reads one handedness class set at t = 0
   (P5). Period-4 of the pi/2 turn means handedness repeats every four turns;
   the open spiral assigns each scale a chirality inherited, never invented.
   Testable claim if law is taken as hypothesis: large-scale spin-handedness
   bias must exist and point one way (see Part V, observable 1).
