CONCEPT: "Photon in a Net"
In nanophotonics, photons can be confined and guided in periodic dielectric structures known as photonic crystals, which act as a "net" for light due to their periodic refractive index variations creating photonic bandgaps. Similarly, plasmonic nanostructures can confine light to sub-wavelength volumes via surface plasmon polaritons.
