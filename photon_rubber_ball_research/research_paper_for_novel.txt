THE LIMITS OF REDUCTIONISM
While it is true that all behavior ultimately derives from
the standard model of particle physics and general relativity,
this knowledge alone does not enable prediction. The
combinatorial explosion of interactions necessitates

not from reduction to fundamentals alone but from

THE MEASUREMENT PROBLEM
At this scale, the observer becomes inseparable from the
observed. To measure position with high precision requires
short-wavelength photons, whose momentum transfer
disturbs the very motion being sought. Energy measurements
are constrained by the natural linewidth set by finite
lifetimes. The standard quantum limit looms—a boundary
on simultaneous precision that no technical ingenuity can
circumvent, only circumvent through quantum nondemolition
techniques or squeezed states.

THE ARROW OF TIME
In the dance of a few hundred atoms, time's arrow flickers.
