# Photon-Sized Rubber Ball Research Project

This folder contains the complete research materials for the "Photon-Sized Rubber Ball: A Multi-Scale Physics Exploration" project.

## Contents

### Research Documents
- `infosheet.html` - Original research paper
- `infosheet_expanded.html` - Expanded research analysis with detailed physics
- `infosheet_expanded_enhanced.html` - Expanded analysis with enhanced references
- `infosett_book.html` - Book-formatted version (published quality)
- `infosett_book_enhanced.html` - Enhanced book version with more references
- `research_findings_expansion_summary.*` - Summary of expanded findings (HTML, MD, PDF)
- `research_paper_findings.txt` - Comprehensive research paper with key findings and analysis
- `research_paper_for_novel.txt` - Narrative-style research paper suitable for literary adaptation

- `work_and_momentum_integrals.txt` - Explanation of work and momentum integrals in photon momentum transfer
