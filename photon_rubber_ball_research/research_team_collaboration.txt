INTEGRATED FINDINGS:
1. Scale Parameter n = 10β serves as a unifying dimensionless quantity across domains
2. The approximation (n² × 5)/2 emerges from first-order Taylor expansion in multiple contexts
3. The fixed point n=0.4 (β=0.04c) represents a scale-transition velocity where:
   - Relativistic effects begin to be measurable in quantum systems
   - Surface effects start to dominate bulk properties in mechanics
   - Thermal fluctuations become comparable to predicted deformation magnitudes
4. Multi-scale framework required: Quantum (<100nm) → Mesoscopic (100-1000nm) → Macroscopic (>1000nm)
