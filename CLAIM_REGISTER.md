Governance doc, added 2026-09-25. The master inventory: **every** claim this
corpus makes, or that it explicitly refuses to make, numbered and
verdict-coded. Companion to PROJECT_INDEX.md (what is here), CORRIGENDUM.md
(what broke and was fixed) — this file is WHOLE-CORPUS claims.

Verdict codes (uniform): [ESTABLISHED] measured/tested/consensus ·
[PROVEN] proved (gate check N or classical theorem) · [CONJECTURE] serious but
unproven · [OPEN] no measurement/consensus yet · [FRAMING] vocabulary/stance,
no science status · [POSTULATE] invented axiom, flagged · [FALSE] proposed and
falsified (corrected record exists) · [NOT-CLAIM] explicitly refused.

Rule: any NEW claim a user or the thread proposes must be added here first.
If it is verifiable in-repo (numeric/arithmetic), it must also become a battery
check before it may appear anywhere else (corrigendum family 25/57 policy).
A claim is a real claim only if it survives to be listed; everything else is a
NOT-claim (section X).
